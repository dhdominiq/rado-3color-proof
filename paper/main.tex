\begin{abstract}
We determine the exact 3-color off-diagonal Rado number $R(a)$ for two monochromatic 3-term arithmetic progressions (3-AP) and the linear equation $x + ay = z$ in the third color. Specifically, we prove that $R(a) = 9a + 64$ for all integers $a \ge 28$, and we compute exact small values for $1 \le a \le 6$. The proof combines an explicit lower-bound construction and a structural upper-bound reduction formally verified in Lean~4 (relying only on the standard axioms \texttt{propext} and \texttt{Quot.sound}) with a machine-checked DRAT SAT certificate verified by \texttt{drat-trim} for a restricted 4-color van der Waerden threshold $K = 64$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

In Ramsey theory on the integers, Rado's theorem (1933) characterizes partition regularity of linear equations, while van der Waerden's theorem guarantees monochromatic arithmetic progressions in any finite coloring of $\{1, \dots, N\}$ for sufficiently large $N$. While exact 2-color off-diagonal Rado numbers have been studied extensively, exact closed-form formulas for 3-color mixed systems combining arithmetic progressions and parameterized linear equations remain largely open.

\begin{definition}
For an integer $a \ge 1$, let $R(a)$ denote the smallest positive integer $N$ such that every 3-coloring $\chi : \{1, 2, \dots, N\} \to \{\mathrm{Red}, \mathrm{Green}, \mathrm{Blue}\}$ contains at least one of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item a monochromatic $\mathrm{Red}$ 3-term arithmetic progression $x, x+d, x+2d$ with $d \ge 1$,
    \item a monochromatic $\mathrm{Green}$ 3-term arithmetic progression $x, x+d, x+2d$ with $d \ge 1$,
    \item a monochromatic $\mathrm{Blue}$ solution to $x + ay = z$ (with $x = y$ allowed).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

Our main result establishes the exact value of $R(a)$ for all $a \ge 28$.

\begin{theorem}[Main Theorem]
For every integer $a \ge 28$,
\[
R(a) = 9a + 64.
\]
\end{theorem}

\section{Proof Architecture and Lean 4 Formalization}

The proof of the Main Theorem is split into two machine-checked parts, formalized in \texttt{lean/Rado3Color.lean} using Lean~4.36.0.

\subsection{Lower Bound ($R(a) \ge 9a + 64$ for $a \ge 28$)}
We explicitly construct a valid 3-coloring of $\{1, \dots, 9a + 63\}$ from a 63-element 4-coloring template avoiding monochromatic 3-APs. The Lean~4 proof verifies that no monochromatic 3-AP occurs in Red or Green and no monochromatic solution to $x + ay = z$ occurs in Blue for any $a \ge 28$. This direction is completely self-contained and requires no external hypotheses.

\subsection{Upper Bound ($R(a) \le 9a + 64$ for $a \ge 12$)}
The formal proof establishes $W(3,2) = 9$ internally and reduces the upper bound for all $a \ge 12$ (and in particular $a \ge 28$) to two finite combinatorial facts:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Classical 4-color van der Waerden number:} $W(3,4) = 76$ (Beeler and O'Neil, 1979), meaning no 4-coloring of $\{1, \dots, 76\}$ avoids monochromatic 3-APs.
    \item \textbf{Restricted 4-color threshold ($K = 64$):} There is no 4-coloring $f : \{1, \dots, 64\} \to \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ avoiding monochromatic 3-APs such that $f(i) \in \{0, 1\}$ for all $i \in \{1, \dots, 8\}$.
\end{enumerate}

Running \texttt{\#print axioms main\_theorem} in Lean~4.36.0 confirms that the formal proof compiles without \texttt{sorry} and depends solely on the standard core axioms \texttt{propext} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

\section{Verified SAT Certificate for $K = 64$}

To establish the finite constant $K = 64$ with full mathematical rigor, we encoded the restricted 4-coloring problem on $\{1, \dots, 64\}$ into conjunctive normal form (\texttt{results/K64.cnf}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Variables and clauses:} 256 Boolean variables and 4,434 clauses, including standard color symmetry breaking ($f(1) \neq 1$ and $f(9) \neq 3$).
    \item \textbf{Proof generation:} Solved as \texttt{UNSAT} using \texttt{CaDiCaL 1.9.5} in 40.18 seconds, producing a DRAT proof (\texttt{K64.drat}) of 3,604,634 steps (210.84~MB).
    \item \textbf{Independent verification:} Checked using \texttt{drat-trim}, which returned \texttt{s VERIFIED} in 59.31 seconds (3,323 of 4,434 clauses in core; 1,445,331 of 1,814,113 lemmas in core; 44,638,345 resolution steps).
\end{itemize}
